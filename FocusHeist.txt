Brief explanation of how FocusHeist works

FocusHeist is like a personal digital study workspace. Instead of students opening many apps, checking notifications, searching for notes, and losing focus, they can keep their important study work in one place.

The basic flow would be:

Open FocusHeist → Select today’s tasks → Start a focus session → Study without distractions → Complete tasks → View progress

For example:

A student opens FocusHeist and sees:
Maths homework — 30 minutes
Physics revision — 45 minutes
English notes — 20 minutes

The student selects Physics and starts a 45-minute Focus Session. During the session, FocusHeist shows only the timer, study goal, and required notes/resources. After finishing, the student marks the task complete and earns progress toward their daily goal.

The app can then show:

Total focused study time
Completed tasks
Subject-wise progress
Daily and weekly focus statistics
Study streaks
Suggestions for unfinished work

For the smart-board mode, a teacher could open FocusHeist and quickly select:

Class → Subject → Chapter → Lesson material

Then the teacher can open a PDF, presentation, video, quiz, or digital worksheet without searching through many folders.

The project’s main goal can be written as:

“FocusHeist is a distraction-free digital learning and classroom-management platform designed to improve students’ focus, organize educational resources, reduce unnecessary paper use, and make smart-classroom learning more efficient.”

The name is also strong because “Heist” gives it a unique gaming-style identity:

Distractions steal your attention. FocusHeist helps you steal it back. 🧠⚡
